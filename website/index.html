How MySpace works

Four simple steps from checkout to your door.

  1. Sign up free

    Create your account and get your personal MySpace ID.

  2. Shop online

    Buy from your favourite stores abroad and ship to our address with your ID.

  3. We consolidate

    We receive, check and pack your parcels together into one shipment.

  4. Delivered to you

    Your box flies to Iraq and is delivered to your door.
